## Our Intentions

CIVITAS/CORE is an open-source project intended to encourage broad adoption, integration, and ecosystem development. The software is therefore distributed under a copyleft open-source license. Please refer to the license for details.

To support wide market adoption and standardization, CIVITAS/CORE is designed as a vendor-neutral software platform, independent of any private-sector company. This approach strengthens digital sovereignty in the public sector and facilitates reuse by ensuring high compatibility across diverse environments where the software can add value.

Governance of the project is exercised exclusively by public-sector organizations, including public-law entities like municipalities or entities owned or controlled by municipalities.

Private-sector companies are encouraged to adopt and use the CIVITAS/CORE trademark in order to promote market adoption. They may contribute to the project through software development and may build commercial services and business offerings based on CIVITAS/CORE.
